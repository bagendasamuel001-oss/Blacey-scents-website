BLACEY SCENTS UG

Made in Kazo, worn around Kampala.

Located at Kazo Muganzi Lwaza, Kampala, Uganda. Blacey Scents We sell oil perfumes and scents for both genders (male&female).

Each blend is composed in small batches, tested for how it opens, settles, and lingers on skin through a Kampala day. We work with oud, amber, vanilla, and native botanicals, balanced against classic florals and citrus for a scent that feels both familiar and entirely new.

We offer competitive prices without compromising on quality,— making premium fragrances affordable for everyone. Ensuring that every bottle meets our exacting standards.
